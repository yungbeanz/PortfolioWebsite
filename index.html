About

A timeline of how I got where I am today.

2000-2012

Early Years

As a kid, I was always extremely creative. My mum would buy me clay to make sculptures out of. I later asked for a guitar, and became very invested in music at an early age. I played music well into my teenage years. Around the age of twelve, I discovered this cool website called Codecademy, and started teaching myself Javascript. This didn't last long, as my attention span was quite short.

2012-2018

Finding my Dream

As a person with ADHD, I was constantly juggling new hobbies back and forth all throughout my teenage years. It was great, because I knew a lot of things, but I was never good at ONE specific thing. Graduation came along, and I had to start thinking of what I wanted to do for a career. There was nothing that specifically struck my mind. I always had shoulder problems, and I really liked the idea of helping others through pain similar to mine, so I decided to enter Kinesiology.

2018-2022

Burning the Midnight Oil

After many long nights of studying and pondering if I had chosen the right path, I felt as though I was missing something in the path I had chosen. I realized that thing was a creative outlet in my chosen career. I almost felt trapped in my schooling, as I was memorizing so many terms, but I was never creating anything original. Creativity has always been extremely important to me.

2022-2024

It Clicked

I remembered when I was a kid and discovered javascript, I felt as though I could bring my creativity into a practical environment. So I switched programs, and gave it a shot. One semester later, here we are, I absolutely love it. After going through Java, and learning an introduction to programming concepts, it felt as though I had struck gold. The opportunity to learn such valuable concepts of programming, is something I feel very honoured and grateful for.
